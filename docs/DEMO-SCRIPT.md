# AIPFA competition demo script

Target runtime: 8 minutes 30 seconds, leaving 90 seconds of margin under the 10-minute limit. Use a synthetic account and synthetic bill only. Do not show real credentials, email, transaction notes, API keys, or personal documents.

## 0:00–0:45 — Opening

“AIPFA is a personal finance assistant for people who need more than a list of transactions. It connects income, expenses, savings, and budgets to explain what is happening and suggest a next step.”

## 0:45–1:45 — Sign in and dashboard

Use a prepared synthetic demo account. Show the dashboard cards, spending categories, daily spending chart, and the period selector. Explain that the accounting period is anchored to the first income date.

## 1:45–2:45 — Transactions and budget

Show the transaction form. Add a small synthetic expense and point out the immediate summary and validation. Show the budget tab and one overspending/daily pace insight. Avoid presenting sample numbers as real user outcomes.

## 2:45–4:00 — Bill scan

Choose a synthetic receipt image. Click Analyze bill. Review the suggested vendor, amount, category, and date. Point out that extraction can be wrong, especially dates and currency, and that the result only fills the form. Correct one field if needed. Click Add only after review.

## 4:00–5:45 — Assistant and report

Ask the assistant for a summary of the synthetic account. Show one personalized observation and explain that the assistant is informational. Generate one PDF or spreadsheet report for a selected range and show its contents.

## 5:45–6:45 — Privacy controls

Open Account settings. Explain that AI chat context and selected bill images go to the configured provider while AI permission is enabled. Turn it off and show that local dashboard/reports remain available while AI calls are blocked. Turn it back on only if the demo needs another AI request.

## 6:45–7:45 — Interaction and architecture

Briefly show voice interaction or PWA install affordance if it works on the demo device. Explain the path: browser → Express API → SQLite; optional AI calls go through OpenRouter; reports are generated by the app.

## 7:45–8:30 — Close

“AIPFA combines transparent budget arithmetic with optional AI interaction. It keeps the user in control: the assistant advises, and the user reviews every transaction before saving.”

## Before recording

- Run the manual test cases and record actual pass/fail results.
- Prepare demo account data in an isolated sandbox database.
- Confirm bill scan, AI permission toggle, report download, and voice behavior on the recording environment.
- Confirm the OpenRouter account has available credits; Azure student credits do not fund OpenRouter requests.
- Do not use a live personal bill or real account. The recording device and deployed environment must use synthetic demo data.
- Remove personal data from the screen and keep the recording below 10 minutes.
